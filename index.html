About

I'm a BTech engineering student who spends most of my time in the space between two disciplines that don't usually share a desk: rigorous technical problem-solving, and creative, visual storytelling. Some days that means modeling a physical system or debugging a piece of code until it behaves exactly as it should; other days it means cutting a project reel or laying out a technical drawing until it reads clearly at a glance.

What ties it together is a habit I picked up early: an idea isn't finished when it works — it's finished when it's legible, to a compiler, to a set of dimensions, or to an audience. That's the standard I try to hold every project to, whether it's built out of code, cardboard, or a timeline in an editor.
